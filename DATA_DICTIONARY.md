# Data dictionary

candidate_counts.csv.gz contains one record per trial, scenario, retention condition and candidate. trial_id identifies a repeated simulation trial; scenario distinguishes primary_no_identity, identity_retained, and legacy_same_release. condition_id is H<hierarchy probability>_A<attribute probability>. candidate_concept_id is an opaque label, not a SNOMED identifier. is_reference marks the sampled hidden reference; is_ancestor marks candidates known to be ancestors of that reference in the source graph. k, e and c are shared, expression-only and candidate-only binary feature counts.

trial_design.csv contains one record per simulated expression. root_concept_id is an opaque seed cluster for the paired bootstrap. n_candidates gives candidate-set size. hierarchy_total/kept and attribute_total/kept describe omission. empty_expression flags zero retained features; reference_identity_retained flags explicit hidden identity; n_features_distinguishing_reference_from_root is a descriptive evidence count. The identity flag for legacy uses the earlier feature namespace and is not a validated legacy self-token indicator.

Opaque concept labels preserve the numeric ordering of the source labels to reproduce bootstrap sampling exactly; the reverse mapping is not included. HSCS trial labels are record labels, not patient identifiers. expected/ stores the frozen exported summaries and percentile bootstrap intervals for validation. The estimates are exploratory, with no multiplicity correction. The legacy scenario uses optimistic competition ranks; corrected scenarios average retrieval metrics over all random orderings of score ties.

The counts-based data reproduce score and rank calculations, all primary and sensitivity summaries, and paired confidence intervals without reconstructing licensed ontology content. They do not independently verify semantic feature construction or clinical appropriateness. Full ontology preprocessing requires the separately obtained licensed RF2 source and reviewed seed inputs. No natural patient text is included.
